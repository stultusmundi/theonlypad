Overall project state

This is an incomplete development build with a substantial working foundation. It is beyond scaffolding: there is a real offline Swift/AppKit application, a local app/archive, meaningful file-failure and recovery coverage, and actual packaged desktop tests. It is not yet a complete Notepad++-style product under the agreed brief.

There is no defensible overall completion percentage yet. A feature group can contain several large requirements. “69 partial” does not mean 91% complete, and zero fully signed-off groups does not mean no useful work has been done. Final validation is deliberately stricter than having code or a passing build.

The matrix was recounted for this report. Its older 67/9 and other dated summaries are historical; the current row data is 69 partial and 7 outstanding. Repository policy, security, test acceptance and reference-parity obligations are additional to those 76 A–I feature groups.

Three distinct states matter: adopted local source, privately tested candidates, and the packaged app. Recent language-manager and aggregate-quit improvements are private candidates; they have not changed the tested package.
